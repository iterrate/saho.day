Run Saho at your institution

Any institution (a library, a college, a panchayat, a hospital) can run its own Saho, for the people it serves or for its own staff.

Libraries

A reading room becomes a place to learn with others. Saho points learners to books on your own shelves.

Panchayats

One Saho can serve every library in a panchayat, with local resources and local priorities.

Colleges

Students and alumni keep learning beyond the syllabus. Your college decides how learning works at its place.

Any organisation

A hospital could run Saho to help its staff keep learning. So could a cooperative, a club or a company.

Your place, your rules

You decide who can join, which AI model the guide uses, and how learning works at your place. You stay connected to every other Saho place, and you can move your place to other hosting at any time.

What it costs

The basic platform is free for learners. Institutions pay Iterrate for a licence, setup, hosting and support, or run Saho themselves.

An institution can add its own courses or fees, and learners can pay for premium features beyond what their institution covers.

Where it stands

Saho is in development, and the first test version is under way. A small pilot with libraries comes next.

If your institution would like to take part, write to us.
