ഒരുമിച്ച് പഠിക്കാംLearn at your own pace, with people near you.

Saho is a guide and a circle for people learning on their own, at any stage of life. It helps you work out what to learn next, and finds you people to learn it with, in Malayalam, English or a mix of both.

How it works For institutions

A guide

Talk things through in the language you think in. The guide suggests next steps and resources, including books on your library’s shelf. It doesn’t lecture.

Circles

Small groups of learners who meet where they are, online and in person. Helpers from the community explain, review and encourage.

Your record

What you learn and what you say belong to you. Places see only what you allow, and you can take it with you.

For finding a direction, not cramming for one exam.

Think of a recent graduate doing part-time work, unsure of what comes next. Saho is for exploring your world, your skills and what’s possible, at your own pace.

The name

Saho is a call to work together, named for Sahodaran Ayyappan and his ideals of fraternity and equality.

saho.day reads as sahoday: rising together.
