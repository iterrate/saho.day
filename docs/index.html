ഒരുമിച്ച് പഠിക്കാംLearn on your own terms, together.

Saho is a guide and a gathering place for people learning on their own, at any stage of life. It helps you work out what to learn next, and finds you people to learn it with, in Malayalam, English or a mix of both.

How it works For institutions

In development. A pilot with libraries comes next. മലയാളത്തിൽ വായിക്കാം

A guide

An AI assistant you can talk things through with, in the language you think in. It suggests next steps and resources, including books on your library’s shelf. It doesn’t lecture.

Circles

Small groups of learners who meet where they are, online or in person. Helpers from the community explain, review and encourage.

Your record

What you learn and what you say belong to you. Places see only what you allow. Take it with you wherever you go, or delete it completely.

For finding your own direction.

Think of a recent graduate doing part-time work, unsure of what comes next. Saho is for exploring your world, your skills and what’s possible, at your own pace.
